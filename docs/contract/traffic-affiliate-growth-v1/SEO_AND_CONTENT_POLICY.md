# Traffic and Affiliate Growth V1 — SEO and Content Policy

Status: `ACTIVE`
Verified: `2026-07-26`

## Search policy

- Follow Google Search Essentials and spam policies
- Use a crawlable 200 response, descriptive unique title/H1, final-host canonical, valid sitemap, and intentional index directives
- A sitemap supports discovery but does not guarantee indexing
- Page Indexing and URL Inspection evidence determine URL-level state
- Search Console trends in impressions and clicks take priority over guessed rank targets
- Reviews must show first-hand evidence, method, limitations, and what was actually tested
- Structured data must match visible content and use valid values

## Content policy

- Write for a named user task, not a broad AI keyword
- Preserve practical inputs, steps, prompts, checks, and failure modes
- Distinguish editorial facts, test results, assumptions, and advertisements
- Do not publish a product review without actual use
- Do not mass-produce thin pages or rewrite all 17 pages without query/page evidence
- Show correction and verification method before claiming editorial trust
