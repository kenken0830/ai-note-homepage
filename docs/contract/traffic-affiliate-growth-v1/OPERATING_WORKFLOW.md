# Traffic and Affiliate Growth V1 — Operating Workflow

Status: `ACTIVE`

## Phase A — Baseline

Verify production, deployment SHA, Search Console evidence, Analytics integration, public URL inventory, index directives, canonical links, sitemap, and scorecard.

## Phase B — Discoverability

Human verifies Search Console access and exports Performance/Pages data. Codex maps query, page, country, and device evidence to the 17 canonical use cases and fixes only demonstrated crawl/canonical issues.

## Phase C — Site clarity

Remove operator-facing homepage sections, state the target reader and next action, show at most five public guides, retain the working free kit, and explain evidence/advertising policy.

## Phase D — Evidence content

After Notta approval, a human runs the frozen synthetic fixture. Preserve raw output, compare with ground truth, score errors and correction effort, mask screenshots, and obtain human evidence approval.

## Phase E — Distribution

Prepare note/X drafts, UTM names, and posting checks. Human approval is required before posting or Search Console submission.

## Phase F — Affiliate pilot

After approval and evidence: register exactly one human-approved destination, show clear PR disclosure, use `rel="sponsored noopener"`, record a controlled click event, merge/publish with human approval, submit the advertising page URL, and reconcile A8.net results.

## Phase G — Iteration

Use 28-day Search Console and Analytics windows. Create a bounded change request, implement, review, and publish with human approval. Expand only themes with verified demand or conversion evidence.
