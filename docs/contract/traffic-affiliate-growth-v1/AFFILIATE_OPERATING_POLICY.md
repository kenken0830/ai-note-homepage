## Scope

Only Notta program `s00000024524001` is in scope. Submission through A8.net is recorded, but approval is pending. No other program, generic registry, ranking, or automatic link conversion is allowed.

## Publication gate

An affiliate link may render only when all are true:

- program approval fact recorded
- actual synthetic-fixture test complete
- evidence manifest complete
- screenshots and conclusion approved by a human
- PR and privacy wording approved
- exact HTTPS destination approved
- exact commit and production action approved

Before that gate, live links and public review count must remain zero.

## Disclosure

The page must make its advertising nature easy to recognize at the opening and near the CTA. The link must use `rel="sponsored noopener"`. Claims must match actual evidence and current official terms.

## Measurement and reconciliation

- `affiliate_click`: controlled click event only
- `affiliate_conversion_reported`: A8.net-reported result
- `affiliate_commission_approved`: A8.net-approved result
- payment receipt: private accounting evidence

Clicks are never substituted for conversions. Tracking destinations and private A8.net identifiers are not stored in analytics or Git.
